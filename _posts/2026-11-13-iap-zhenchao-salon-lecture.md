I was invited to give a lecture at the **Zhenchao Salon** of the Institute of Atmospheric Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences (IAP), where I shared how **AI agents** can assist the development of forecast systems, including how agents can help develop a general circulation model (GCM). The project from the talk is [PlaSiC](/PlaSiC/index.html), and the recording is available on Bilibili.

我应邀在<strong>中国科学院大气物理研究所“震潮沙龙”</strong>作讲座，分享了如何利用 <strong>AI 智能体</strong>辅助预报系统开发，包括如何借助智能体辅助开发环流模式（GCM）。讲座涉及的项目是 <a href="/PlaSiC/index.html">PlaSiC</a>，讲座录像已发布在 B 站。
